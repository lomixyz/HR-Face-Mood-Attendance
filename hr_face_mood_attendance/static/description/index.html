Face & Mood Attendance

Camera-based check-in/out that recognizes an employee's face and reads their mood at the same moment — so HR can step in before a quiet bad week turns into a resignation.

How it works

Three steps — nothing leaves the employee's browser except the final result.

1
Look at the camera
The employee stands in front of the kiosk (or opens the self check-in page). Their face is matched entirely in the browser — no photo is ever uploaded.
2
Mood is read at that moment
The dominant facial expression (happy, sad, neutral, ...) is captured at the same instant and logged on the attendance record.
3
HR is alerted, not the algorithm
After enough consecutive low-mood days, HR and the manager get a review task. A person always decides — nothing is auto-approved.

Key features

👤 In-browser face recognition
Runs entirely client-side with face-api.js. No external service, no photo ever leaves the browser — only the match result is sent.
😊 Mood at every check-in/out
Captures the dominant facial expression at check-in and check-out and stores it on the attendance record for reporting.
🔔 Wellbeing streak alerts
A daily job watches for consecutive low-mood days per employee and raises a human-review task for HR and the manager — never an automatic decision.
✅ Explicit, revocable consent
Nothing is captured or matched until an employee's consent is ticked on their record. Withdrawing consent deletes their stored face data immediately.
🏢 Multi-company ready
Kiosk model URL, thresholds and incentive amount are configurable per company from Settings.
🌐 Arabic included
Full Arabic translation of fields, views, menus and messages, ready to use as soon as Arabic is enabled.

⚙️ Configuration

  1. Install the module (depends on Attendances and Time Off).
  2. Go to Settings > Companies > (your company) and open the Face & Mood Attendance section: set the sad-day threshold, confidence threshold and kiosk model URL.
  3. Add a dedicated user to the Mood Attendance Kiosk Device group and leave it logged in at the kiosk page on the entrance tablet.
  4. Each employee ticks consent on their record, then enrolls their own face from Attendances > My Face ID — done.
